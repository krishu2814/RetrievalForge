# NovaTech Solutions: Enterprise Security & Compliance Architecture

NovaTech builds security into every layer of our retrieval and inference pipelines. We are audited annually by independent third-party assessors.

1. Certifications and Accreditations:
- SOC 2 Type II Certified: Covers Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust principles.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certified for information security management systems.
- HIPAA Compliant: We sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with eligible Business and Enterprise tier healthcare customers.

2. Encryption Standards:
- Data in Transit: Enforced TLS 1.3 encryption across all public endpoints and internal service-to-service communication. Older cipher suites and TLS 1.1/1.0 are permanently disabled.
- Data at Rest: Encrypted using AES-256 with customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) via AWS KMS or HashiCorp Vault.
- Vector Embeddings Storage: Vector indexes in FAISS and Qdrant are encrypted at rest with per-tenant isolation keys.

3. Zero-Retention Model & AI Privacy:
- Customer queries, retrieved document context, and generated answers are NEVER used to train, fine-tune, or improve any shared public foundation models.
- Enterprise customers can enable Zero Data Retention (ZDR) mode: prompts and responses are held strictly in ephemeral RAM and wiped immediately following completion of the streaming session.

4. Network Security & Perimeter:
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) mitigation and Web Application Firewall (WAF) managed via Cloudflare Enterprise.
- Dedicated VPC peering and AWS PrivateLink available for Enterprise tier clients to keep AI retrieval traffic completely off the public Internet.
