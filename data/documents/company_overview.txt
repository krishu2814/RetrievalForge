# NovaTech Solutions: Company Overview

NovaTech Solutions Inc. is a cloud software enterprise founded in 2021, headquartered in San Francisco, California. We specialize in enterprise generative artificial intelligence, intelligent search infrastructure, and automated customer experience systems.

Our mission is to empower organizations to operationalize AI safely, reliably, and transparently. Our flagship platform, NovaAI Enterprise Suite, provides natural language assistants, document intelligence pipelines, and real-time knowledge retrieval.

Key Business Units:
1. NovaAI Platform: Core enterprise foundation models, fine-tuning infrastructure, and agent orchestration.
2. KnowledgeForge Engine: Hybrid enterprise search and retrieval infrastructure for corporate data.
3. Customer Experience Cloud: Omnichannel AI customer support agents with human-in-the-loop escalation.

Corporate Values:
- Transparency and Observability: We believe in explainable AI systems where every output is traceable to source data.
- Security-First: Data privacy, zero-retention options, and end-to-end encryption across all tenant boundaries.
- Continuous Innovation: Pushing retrieval-augmented generation forward through hybrid indexing and reranking.

Offices and Global Presence:
NovaTech maintains offices in San Francisco, New York, London, and Bengaluru, supporting over 1,200 enterprise customers worldwide across financial services, healthcare, e-commerce, and software.
