# NovaTech Solutions: Physical Hardware Return Policy

This policy governs physical appliance returns for NovaTech Edge Appliance devices (NovaEdge-500 and NovaEdge-Enterprise). This is distinct from software subscription refunds.

1. Physical Return Window:
Physical hardware devices may be returned within 45 calendar days of recorded carrier delivery. Devices returned after 45 days will be rejected and shipped back at the customer's expense.

2. Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA):
- Before shipping any device, customers must request an RMA tracking number by emailing hardware-ops@novatech.io.
- Packages received without a clearly visible RMA number on the exterior label will be refused at our warehouse.

3. Condition of Returned Units:
- All original packaging, power adapters, rack-mounting brackets, and fiber optic transceivers must be returned undamaged.
- Devices exhibiting cosmetic damage, liquid spills, or unauthorized enclosure tampering will incur a 20% restocking and refurbishment fee.
- Defective hardware covered under our 3-year hardware warranty will be replaced with expedited shipping within 48 hours at no charge.

4. Return Shipping:
NovaTech provides prepaid shipping labels for domestic returns within the United States and European Union. Customers in other jurisdictions are responsible for return transit and customs duties.
