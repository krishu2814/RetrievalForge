# NovaTech Solutions: Customer Billing & Invoicing Policy

This policy outlines invoicing cycles, payment terms, billing disputes, and account delinquency rules.

1. Billing Frequency & Invoicing:
- Monthly subscriptions are charged automatically on the recurring calendar date of initial account signup.
- Annual contracts are invoiced 30 days prior to contract renewal with Net 30 payment terms.
- Invoices are delivered electronically via PDF to the designated billing email contact and accessible in the customer portal.

2. Accepted Payment Methods:
- Major Credit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover.
- Automated Clearing House (ACH) direct debit for US accounts with over $500 monthly spend.
- International Wire Transfers: Supported for Business and Enterprise tiers on annual commitments. Checks and cryptocurrency are not accepted.

3. Payment Grace Period & Late Fees:
- A 14-day grace period is granted following a failed recurring transaction or past-due invoice.
- During the grace period, automated email notifications are dispatched at days 1, 3, 7, and 12.
- If payment is not received within 14 days, a late fee equal to 1.5% per month (or maximum allowable by law) is assessed, and API access is temporarily paused.
- Accounts past due for more than 45 calendar days are referred to collections and customer data is scheduled for purging per our Data Privacy Policy.

4. Billing Inquiries & Disputes:
- Customers must notify NovaTech of any billing discrepancies or dispute claims within 60 calendar days of invoice date by emailing billing@novatech.io.
- Failure to dispute an invoice within 60 days constitutes full acceptance of the assessed charges.
