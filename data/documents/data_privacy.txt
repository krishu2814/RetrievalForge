# NovaTech Solutions: Global Data Privacy & Retention Policy

NovaTech complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the UK Data Protection Act.

1. Data Retention Timelines:
- System Access and Audit Logs: Retained for exactly 90 calendar days, after which they are automatically expunged from cold storage archives.
- User Profile Information: Retained for the active duration of the customer subscription plus 30 days post-cancellation.
- Inactive Vector Database Embeddings: If an account is closed or downgraded, customer vector index files are marked for deletion and permanently purged within 14 calendar days.

2. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):
Customers or individual data subjects may submit a verifiable erasure request to privacy@novatech.io. Upon identity verification:
- All raw documents in object storage buckets are deleted within 7 business days.
- Vector database entries and sparse BM25 indices are re-indexed to expunge associated chunk IDs within 48 hours.
- Automated confirmation receipts with cryptographic SHA-256 deletion certificates are provided to the requestor.

3. Data Processing Agreements (DPAs):
We provide standard pre-signed GDPR DPAs incorporating European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for cross-border international data transfers.

4. Data Protection Officer (DPO):
Inquiries regarding privacy, regulatory audits, or sub-processor lists may be directed to our appointed Data Protection Officer at dpo@novatech.io.
