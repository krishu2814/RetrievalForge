# NovaTech Solutions: Employee Leave & Time-Off Policy

This internal policy applies to all full-time and part-time salaried employees of NovaTech globally.

1. Paid Time Off (PTO) / Annual Vacation:
- Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued on a monthly pro-rata basis (1.67 days per month).
- A maximum of 5 unused vacation days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year. Any additional unused days expire on March 31st.
- Vacation requests exceeding 3 consecutive working days require managerial approval submitted at least 2 weeks in advance via Workday.

2. Sick and Wellness Leave:
- Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, credited on January 1st.
- For absences exceeding 3 consecutive calendar days due to illness, a physician's medical certificate must be submitted to HR Benefits.

3. Parental and Family Care Leave:
- Primary and secondary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following the qualifying event and may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks.

4. Bereavement Leave:
- 5 paid days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- 3 paid days for extended family members (grandparent, in-laws).
