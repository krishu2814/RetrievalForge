# NovaTech Solutions: Customer Refund Policy (Active Version 2.0)

Effective Date: January 15, 2026. This is the current, active refund policy for all NovaTech products and services.

1. Refund Period:
Customers may request a complete refund within 30 calendar days of initial subscription purchase or annual contract renewal. Any refund request received within the 30-day window is entitled to a 100% money-back guarantee with zero cancellation penalties.

2. Eligibility Criteria:
- Applicable to Starter, Pro, and Business tier subscriptions.
- API credit consumption must not exceed 25,000 tokens or 10,000 API calls in the current billing cycle. If usage exceeds this threshold, a pro-rated refund is issued for the unused subscription duration.
- Free trial accounts converting to paid tiers have the standard 30-day window from the date of the first billing charge.

3. Processing and Payout:
- Once approved by the support team, refunds are processed and credited to the customer's original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- For international wire transfers, banking partner delays may require up to 10 business days.
- No administrative or cancellation fees are deducted under Version 2.0.

4. Non-Refundable Items:
- Custom Enterprise SLA add-ons, professional migration consulting, and third-party marketplace integrations are non-refundable once work has commenced.
- Enterprise multi-year negotiated master services agreements (MSAs) are governed by the custom termination clauses specified in their respective contracts.

5. How to Request:
To initiate a refund, submit a ticket to billing-support@novatech.io with the subject line "Refund Request - [Account ID]". Include invoice number and reason for cancellation.
