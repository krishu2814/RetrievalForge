# NovaTech Solutions: Corporate Expense & Travel Policy

This policy governs business expenditures incurred by employees in the performance of company duties.

1. Travel and Lodging Guidelines:
- Flights: Economy class is mandatory for domestic flights under 5 hours. Business class may be booked for international flights exceeding 7 hours with VP approval.
- Hotel Accommodations: Standard business room rates up to $220/night in standard cities, or up to $300/night in designated high-cost metropolitan areas (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo).

2. Meals and Incidentals Per-Diem:
- Daily meal allowance is capped at $75 per day during domestic business travel ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- Itemized receipts are strictly mandatory for all individual meal charges exceeding $25. Alcoholic beverages are limited to one drink with dinner.

3. Home Office and Technology Stipend:
- New hires receive a one-time remote setup stipend of $800 to purchase ergonomic monitors, desks, or accessories.
- Monthly broadband internet reimbursement of up to $60/month is available for remote and hybrid team members.

4. Reimbursement Filing Deadlines:
- All expense reports must be submitted via Expensify within 30 calendar days of incurring the expense.
- Reports submitted later than 60 days will be rejected and ineligible for corporate reimbursement.
