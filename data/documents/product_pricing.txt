# NovaTech Solutions: Subscription Pricing & Tiers (2026 Edition)

All pricing plans are billed either monthly or annually. Annual commitments receive a 20% discount on listed rates.

1. Starter Tier ($49 / month):
- Includes 1,000,000 input tokens and 250,000 output tokens per month.
- Up to 5 team members.
- Vector database storage up to 50,000 document chunks.
- Standard email support with 48-hour response SLA.
- Standard BM25 and dense retrieval with FAISS.

2. Pro Tier ($199 / month):
- Includes 5,000,000 input tokens and 1,200,000 output tokens per month.
- Up to 25 team members.
- Vector database storage up to 250,000 document chunks.
- Priority email and Slack support with 12-hour response SLA.
- Advanced Hybrid search (Dense + BM25 + Reciprocal Rank Fusion).
- Cross-encoder reranking included.

3. Business Tier ($699 / month):
- Includes 20,000,000 input tokens and 5,000,000 output tokens per month.
- Up to 100 team members.
- Up to 1,000,000 document chunks.
- 4-hour critical response SLA, 24/7 emergency incident coverage.
- Custom metadata filtering, automated contextual compression, and MultiQuery retrieval.
- Role-based access control (RBAC) and Single Sign-On (SSO via Okta / SAML 2.0).

4. Enterprise Tier (Custom Quote - Starts at $2,500 / month):
- Unlimited tokens and dedicated inference clusters.
- Custom VPC deployment (AWS, GCP, Azure, or on-premise Kubernetes).
- Dedicated customer success manager and 99.99% uptime guarantee.
- Full compliance suite (HIPAA BAA, SOC 2 Type II, FedRAMP In-Process).

Overage Rates:
Tokens exceeding tier allotments are billed at $1.50 per 1,000,000 input tokens and $4.00 per 1,000,000 output tokens.
