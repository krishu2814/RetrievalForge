# NovaTech Solutions: Customer Refund Policy (Archived Version 1.0)

NOTICE: This is an archived policy document superseded by Version 2.0 on January 15, 2026.

1. Refund Period:
Under Version 1.0, customers could request a full refund within 14 calendar days of initial purchase or subscription renewal. Requests submitted after 14 days were strictly non-refundable.

2. Eligibility Requirements:
- The customer must have consumed less than 5,000 API credits during the billing cycle.
- The account must not have been suspended for Terms of Service violations.
- Monthly subscriptions were eligible for refund within 14 days, but annual contracts were non-refundable after 7 days.

3. Processing Timeline:
Approved refund requests were processed within 10 to 14 business days via the original payment method (Credit Card or Wire Transfer).
Late refund disputes were subject to a $25 administrative processing charge.

4. Exceptions:
Custom enterprise onboarding services, implementation consulting, and dedicated cluster provisioning fees were strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.
