# NovaTech Solutions: Customer Support Service Level Agreement (SLA)

NovaTech provides tiered technical support to ensure enterprise customer continuity and issue resolution.

1. Support Channels and Operating Hours:
- Standard Email Support (support@novatech.io): Available Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.
- Priority Slack Connect Channel: Available for Pro, Business, and Enterprise customers.
- 24/7 Telephone Hotline (+1-800-555-NOVA): Exclusively reserved for Severity 1 outages affecting Business and Enterprise accounts.

2. Severity Levels & First Response Time SLAs:
- Severity 1 (Critical Outage - Core API or Search Down):
  - Starter: 8 business hours
  - Pro: 2 hours (24/7)
  - Business: 30 minutes (24/7)
  - Enterprise: 15 minutes guaranteed with immediate engineering escalation
- Severity 2 (Major Feature Degraded - Fallback operational):
  - Starter: 24 business hours
  - Pro: 8 business hours
  - Business: 2 business hours
  - Enterprise: 1 hour
- Severity 3 (Minor Bug / General Inquiry / Feature Request):
  - All Tiers: 1 to 2 business days

3. Escalation Path:
If a Severity 1 incident is not resolved within 2 hours of initial response, the ticket automatically escalates to the Tier 3 Principal Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) lead and Vice President of Infrastructure.
